International Guests

Your Guide to Manila

Flying in from abroad? We've put together some tips to help you make the most of your time in the Philippines. Manila is a vibrant, welcoming city — we hope you love it as much as we do!

✈️

Getting to Manila

Most international flights arrive at Ninoy Aquino International Airport (NAIA). There are four terminals — check which one your airline uses when booking.

💡 Use Grab (the local rideshare app) from the airport. Avoid unlicensed taxis — always use metered cabs or Grab for safety.
  • Terminal 1: Most international carriers
  • Terminal 2: Philippine Airlines (some intl)
  • Terminal 3: Cebu Pacific, AirAsia, others
  • Terminal 4: Smaller domestic operators
🚗

Getting Around

Manila traffic can be heavy, especially during rush hours. Plan extra travel time!

  • Grab — like Uber, widely available, cashless, reliable. Download before you arrive.
  • Hotel transfers — many hotels offer airport pickup.
  • Taxis — use metered taxis only.
⏰ Allow plenty of time on the wedding day — traffic in Makati/Taguig can be unpredictable. Leave your hotel earlier than you think you need to.
🏨

Recommended Hotels

Conveniently located near Makati and Taguig, close to both venues:

  • Raffles Makati — Luxury, Makati CBD
  • Shangri-La BGC — Upscale, Bonifacio Global City
  • Seda BGC — Modern, mid-range, excellent value
  • Makati Shangri-La — Classic, central Makati

Book early — January is peak season!

💳

Currency & Practical Tips

  • Currency: Philippine Peso (PHP). ATMs widely available.
  • SIM cards: Pick up a local SIM at the airport (Globe or Smart).
  • GCash: Digital wallet widely used in the Philippines.
  • Grab: Download and set up before or right after landing.
  • Power: Type A/B plugs (same as US), 220V.
  • Language: English is widely spoken.
☀️

Weather in January

Great news — you've picked the perfect time to visit!

🌤️ January is dry season in the Philippines. Expect warm, sunny days (~27°C / 80°F) with low humidity and minimal rain.
  • Average temperature: 24–29°C (75–84°F)
  • Very low chance of rain
  • Light, breathable clothing recommended
  • Evenings can be slightly cooler — bring a light layer for air-conditioned venues
🌺

While You're Here

Make the most of your visit to the Philippines!

  • Intramuros — Manila's historic walled city
  • BGC (Bonifacio Global City) — Modern dining, art, and shopping
  • Greenbelt & Glorietta — Iconic Makati malls and restaurants
  • Tagaytay — Scenic volcano views, 1.5 hrs from Manila
  • Palawan or Boracay — World-class beaches for those extending their stay
